A2 (R1-M2): signal vs a permutation null (lib/eric_a2.py; seed 20261015; permutation streams are separate SeedSequences). Real statistic = raw Pearson coexpression: xCell = mean over subjects (>=20 cells) of per-subject r; xSubject/xBulk = Pearson across subjects/samples of Eric's transformed data.
Null = each gene's expression vector permuted independently (within subject for xCell, across subjects/samples for xSubject/xBulk), P permutations (xCell full 10, xSubject 50, xBulk 20, matched xCell 20 (S1) / 10 (S2)). Reported per group: fraction of real positive correlations above the null maximum (over all pairs and permutations) and of real negative ones below the null minimum, plus fractions above/below the null 99.9/99.99/99.999% quantiles (pooled 300k random null pairs per permutation).
Files: results_full.tsv (xCell, all cells, 39 groups), results_xsubject.tsv (39), results_xbulk.tsv (rosmap, velmeshev: full and matched to 48/41 samples, 10 draws), results_S1.tsv (xCell with N_subj pooled cells, 10 draws x 39 groups), results_S2.tsv (xCell, per-subject cap, 3 draws; pending), all_levels.tsv; replicability_with_without_filter*.tsv (A2 filter = keep only pairs beyond the null max/min).
Findings (means over groups): above null MAX, positive: xCell full 0.37% (max 4.7%; null max 0.15, real max 0.61), xSubject 0.02% (null max 0.93, real max 0.98), xBulk ROSMAP (636 samples) 0.03%, Velmeshev (41) 0%; negative below null MIN: xCell 0.9%, xSubject 0.03%, ROSMAP 4.8%.
Above the null 99.9% QUANTILE: xCell full 2.9%, xSubject 9.5%, ROSMAP bulk 48.7%, Velmeshev bulk 6.8% -- so the two criteria rank xCell vs xSubject differently: xCell has ~15x more pairs beyond the extreme-value (max) bar, xSubject more beyond a 99.9% quantile bar. Per-subject xCell correlations are tiny and noisy (mean r across subjects of a sparse single-nucleus signal), so xCell's strength is not a larger share of 'significant' pairs but the reproducibility of its top-ranked edges (A1/baseline).
At matched sample size: xCell with N_subj pooled cells (S1; median 37 cells): null max 1.0 (sparse genes give exact r=1 under permutation), real max 1.0, no real correlation passes in any group; xBulk matched to 48/41 samples: same (null max ~1.0). The max-based criterion is therefore uninformative at the sample sizes of the xSubject level (20-66 samples) for ~10^7-10^8 gene pairs.
A2 filter (keep only pairs beyond the full-size null max/min): xSubject leaves too few pairs (211 of 216 replicability comparisons undefined), xCell 114 of 216 defined with median OR 30 vs 18 unfiltered; see replicability_with_without_filter_summary.tsv.

A2 on S2 draws (results_S2.tsv; 3 draws, 10 permutations each, per group): fraction of positive edges (real r > 0) above the permutation null max: mean 0.02% (max 0.68% in a group); negative below null min mean 0.04%; fraction above the 99.9% null quantile mean 0.8%. Null max averages 0.51 (up to 1.0 in small groups). Under matched cells per subject almost no edge exceeds the null.
